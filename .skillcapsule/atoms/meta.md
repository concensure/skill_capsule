# Meta Skill Capsule: Evolution & Monitoring

## Meta Monitor Usage <!-- @id: meta-monitor-usage -->
Tracks which skills are called together.
Used to identify high-friction workflows and suggest composite atoms.

## Meta Propose Composite <!-- @id: meta-propose-composite -->
Analyzes tracked patterns and generates patch proposals for new composite capsules.
Includes logic for exception management (skipping the composite hook when specified).
